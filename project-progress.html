About This Data
  • Source: each project's Google Sheet shoot tracker. The Studio Master Production Tracker reads all of them twice a day on weekdays (about 9:30 and 2:30), and this page shows that reading. The "as of" time is at the top.
  • The project list (names, SMS IDs, Active / On Hold / Stalled) comes from the Studio Master Production Tracker.
  • "Last Slack numbers" means the project has no shoot tracker yet, so it shows the final count from its old Slack List. Slack Lists were retired on Nov 3, 2026.
  • Overdue counts unticked tasks whose Due / End date has passed. If a date is wrong, fix it in the tracker and it updates on the next reading.
  • A red notice at the top means the tracker reading is late or missing, so the numbers may be behind. The trackers themselves are always current.
  • Task-level detail (owners, dates, notes) lives in each tracker, not here. How to use your tracker →
