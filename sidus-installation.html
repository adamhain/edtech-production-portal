What Is Sidus Link?

Sidus Link is Aputure's lighting-control app — free for Bluetooth control of Aputure/amaran fixtures, or upgraded to Sidus Link Pro (iPad/Mac) for DMX and wireless CRMX control across any manufacturer's fixtures once paired with Sidus hardware like the Sidus One transmitter. This is the app/hardware pairing the studio uses for lighting control in shoots that need more than on-fixture Bluetooth range or per-light adjustment — patching, cues, and multi-light "looks" all live in the app instead of walking to each fixture.
