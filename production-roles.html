Project Workflow

Production Lead roles & expectations.

EdTech's standard project intake and lifecycle — what the Project Manager (PM), Instructional Design (ID) Lead, and Production Lead each own at every phase, from first consult request through retrospective. Source: "EdTech Standard Project Intake and Expectations" (Google Doc).

04
Execution & Production
During the project — broken out by role below
PMID LeadProduction Lead
⌄
RoleDutyDetails
PM Constraint Management Actively conduct overall project management by monitoring budget, timeline, and available resources to keep the project on track.
Meeting FacilitationCheck progress asynchronously, weekly if needed and as outlined in the communication agreements, capturing action items and tracking progress.
Blocker RemovalServe as the primary point of escalation for logistical issues, ensuring the Production and ID teams have what they need from Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to proceed.
ID Lead Content Development Transform SME materials into structured educational materials, starting in the Learning Design Document (LDD). Work with Production Lead to create storyboard and scripts.
Pedagogical AlignmentReview all media and content being produced to ensure alignment with the learning objectives agreed upon in the Project Charter.
LMS IntegrationBuild out the actual course within the Learning Management System (e.g., Canvas), inputting text, organizing modules, and placing media assets.
Script Writing and SupervisionJoint with Production LeadWith Production Lead, determine at the start of the project the timeline and specific deliverables required from the client regarding the script. Jointly review script progress throughout the life of the project. Attend shoots to ensure the script is fully covered, or work with the Production Lead to designate a script supervisor. Report to the PM and project team if the script is over 2 weeks behind schedule per timeline; work with the Production Lead to adjust the project timeline with each delay and communicate the new timeline to the PM and team.
Production Lead Media Execution Direct shoots, record audio, create animations, or develop interactive media elements as defined by the storyboards. Responsible for the project management of the production assets/aspects of the project, including the timeline, deliverable management, and client communication regarding production.
Review Management (ftrack)Upload media assets to the ftrack management system, generate review links, and ensure feedback from the ID Lead and SMEs is integrated into the next version.
Script Writing and SupervisionJoint with ID LeadWith ID Lead, determine at the start of the project the timeline and specific deliverables required from the client regarding the script. Jointly review script progress throughout the life of the project. Jointly determine specifically who will be the script supervisor for each shoot. Report to the PM and project team if the script is over 2 weeks behind schedule per timeline; work with the ID Lead to adjust the project timeline with each delay and communicate the new timeline to the PM and team.
Quality & Accessibility AssuranceEnsure all media files use proper naming conventions, maintain version control, and meet strict accessibility standards (e.g., proper color contrast, captioning).
