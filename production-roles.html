Project Workflow

Production Lead roles & expectations.

EdTech's standard project intake and lifecycle — what the Project Manager (PM), Instructional Design (ID) Lead, and Production Lead each own at every phase, from first consult request through retrospective. Source: "EdTech Standard Project Intake and Expectations" (Google Doc).

Project Phases

Click any phase to expand
01
Pre-Consultation & Intake
Handled entirely by the Project Manager (PM)
PM
⌄
Handle Incoming Requests: The PM posts the consult request to the Slack channel, including a link to the Podio form, and tags expected invitees.
Send Receipts: The PM responds with a Consult Submission Receipt.
Schedule Consult: The PM responds within 2 business days of the submission with a Calendly link to schedule the consultation.
Notify Attendees: The PM sends the Consultation Appointment Notification with the agenda and expectations.
Set Up Digital Workspace: The PM creates a consult Google folder with a Documents sub-folder.
Create Project Documents: The PM creates the Project Design Binder, the Consult Agenda, and the project Learning Design Document (LDD).
Send Invites: The PM creates the consult calendar invite, adds necessary EdTech team members, and creates a second 30-minute follow-up invite for an EdTech debrief.
Follow Up: The PM summarizes the consult and sends the follow-up email with the linked LDD template.
02
Pre-Prioritization
Handled entirely by the PM
PM
⌄
Estimate Costs: The PM creates a good-faith estimate and project scope to ensure adequate funding from the client team and inform prioritization.
Client Communication: The PM follows up with the client to share the good-faith estimate and outline expectations for next steps.
Submit Project: The PM adds the project to the next prioritization docket.
Schedule Follow-ups: As needed, PM schedules a follow-up client meeting and creates the corresponding agenda.
03
Project Initiation & Setup
Post-Prioritization — PM, ID Lead, and Production Lead
PMID LeadProduction Lead
⌄
Communicate Decisions: The PM sends the official project acceptance or rejection email to the client.
Transition Workspaces: The PM converts the consult Google drive into the project drive.
Create Communication Channels: The PM creates the project Slack channel, ensuring the Production Lead and ID Lead are included.
Schedule Initial Meetings: The PM schedules the Internal Handoff, the Client Logistics Meeting, and the Client Meet and Greet.
Attend Initial Meetings: The ID Lead and Production Lead attend the Internal Handoff, Client Logistics Meeting, and Client Meet and Greet.
Schedule Kick-offs: The ID Lead schedules the Internal Kick-off (with all lifetime team members) and the main Project Kick-off.
Finalize Scope & Charter: The ID Lead and PM jointly finalize the Scope of Work and Project Charter, and schedule a meeting with Teggin to finalize them if needed.
Obtain Sign-offs: The ID Lead, Production Lead, and PM jointly secure official sign-off on the Project Charter.
04
Execution & Production
During the project — broken out by role below
PMID LeadProduction Lead
⌄
Project Manager (PM)
Constraint Management: Actively conduct overall project management by monitoring budget, timeline, and available resources to keep the project on track.
Meeting Facilitation: Check progress asynchronously, weekly if needed and as outlined in the communication agreements, capturing action items and tracking progress.
Blocker Removal: Serve as the primary point of escalation for logistical issues, ensuring the Production and ID teams have what they need from Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to proceed.
Instructional Designer (ID Lead)
Content Development: Transform SME materials into structured educational materials, starting in the Learning Design Document (LDD). Work with Production Lead to create storyboard and scripts.
Pedagogical Alignment: Review all media and content being produced to ensure alignment with the learning objectives agreed upon in the Project Charter.
LMS Integration: Build out the actual course within the Learning Management System (e.g., Canvas), inputting text, organizing modules, and placing media assets.
Script Writing and Supervision: With Production Lead, determine at the start of the project the timeline and specific deliverables required from the client regarding the script. Jointly review script progress throughout the life of the project. Attend shoots to ensure the script is fully covered, or work with the Production Lead to designate a script supervisor. Report to the PM and project team if the script is over 2 weeks behind schedule per timeline; work with the Production Lead to adjust the project timeline with each delay and communicate the new timeline to the PM and team.
Production Lead
Media Execution: Direct shoots, record audio, create animations, or develop interactive media elements as defined by the storyboards. The Production Lead is responsible for the project management of the production assets/aspects of the project, including the timeline, deliverable management, and client communication regarding production.
Review Management (ftrack): Upload media assets to the ftrack management system, generate review links, and ensure feedback from the ID Lead and SMEs is integrated into the next version.
Script Writing and Supervision: With ID Lead, determine at the start of the project the timeline and specific deliverables required from the client regarding the script. Jointly review script progress throughout the life of the project. Jointly determine specifically who will be the script supervisor for each shoot. Report to the PM and project team if the script is over 2 weeks behind schedule per timeline; work with the ID Lead to adjust the project timeline with each delay and communicate the new timeline to the PM and team.
Quality & Accessibility Assurance: Ensure all media files use proper naming conventions, maintain version control, and meet strict accessibility standards (e.g., proper color contrast, captioning).
05
Post-Project & Closeout
ID Lead, Production Lead, and PM
ID LeadProduction LeadPM
⌄
Send Closeout Communications: The ID Lead sends the Client Retrospective Form and the final closeout email.
Schedule Retrospectives: The ID Lead schedules both the Client Retrospective and the Internal Retrospective meetings.
Evaluate Conference Potential: The ID Lead identifies if the project is worthy of conference proposals or research.
Organize Digital Assets: The ID Lead and Production Lead collaborate to organize the final Google Drive.
Archive Media: The Production Lead (and team) archives media assets to the Media Library (both in Google Drive and on Server) and identifies candidates for reuse on other projects.
Manage Client Access: The Production team adds everything SME-facing (final scripts, sign-offs, assets) to the final work folder and removes their access from all other project folders.
Develop Marketing Materials: The ID Lead and Production Lead (with assistance from the PM) create the Internal Marketing Documentation/Marketing Packet, as needed.
Coordinate Marketing Launch: The ID Lead and Production Lead determine various needs for the marketing document, and submit a communication request to TDS or Stanford Healthcare alongside Teggin.
Administrative Closeout: The PM closes out the billing processes, Podio records, project Slack channel, and Service Now record.
06
Retrospective
All team members — ID, Production, PM
All Team
⌄
Prepare Feedback: All team members (ID, Production, PM) review "Start, Stop, Continue" feedback for their specific processes.
General Participation: All internal participants attend and participate in actionable conversation, provide shoutouts for team members, and review project goals/objectives.
